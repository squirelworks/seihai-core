Drawing other players from what the server sent, for the flat view Ato and Issho share.

## Breaking changes

- **`client::Other` has three more fields**: `sprite` (prop 17), `status` (prop 18) and `looks` (`OtherLooks`: head, body, colours, sword, shield, GANI and attributes). A struct literal of `Other` needs them; `Other` is now `Default`, so `..Default::default()` fills them.

## Client

- The network thread hands the view each other player's looks, sprite and status with their position, so a client can draw them from their GANI instead of as a marker (seihai #443).

## seihai-draw

- `PixelBuffer::fill_rect` and `PixelBuffer::stroke_rect`: a clipped filled rectangle and a one-pixel outline, for markers and link outlines.
