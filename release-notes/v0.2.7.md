Two iPhone rows of `KNOWN_TOKENS` follow a wider read of 20 iPhone builds from the same publisher (Graal_analysis `client-internals/iphone-version-tokens.md`). The token names the engine generation, not the app.

## Changes

- **`G3D18010`** moves from L to B: Cocoto Kart HD 1.1, Ace Tennis HD 1.0, Bomber Online 1.40 and Dino Smash 1.4 send it (the 2010 engine; gs2lib's "iPhone 1.5").
- **`G3D04048`** is relabelled as the 2008 engine: Belote 1.2/1.50 and 12 more builds of 2008-2010 send it (gs2lib's "iPhone 1.1").

No API changes.
