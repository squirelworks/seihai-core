The combat and respawn rules of the 1.x and 2.17+ clients follow a new read of their binaries (Graal_analysis #444).

## Combat

- **Hit packets in 1.x.** The clients from 1.31 on handle `PLO_HURTPLAYER` (40). Before, Seihai decoded no packet hits before 2.00. 1.21, 1.28 beta 2 and 1.3 test still decode none, because their clients send id 40 to the default case. The handler changed in steps:
  - **1.31 to 1.34:** no range test and no cap (`COMBAT_131`, `COMBAT_134`).
  - **1.35 to 1.39:** a cap of one heart and no range test (`COMBAT_139`).
  - **1.41 rev 1 and NewMain 1.42:** the 2.00 handler, with the 10-tile range and the one-heart cap (`COMBAT_141`, and `COMBAT_142` is now `COMBAT_200`).
  - None of them tests AP 100 on a packet hit.
  - A range or cap of `u8::MAX` means the build has no such test.
- **2.17.1, 2.22 and 2.31** (`COMBAT_217`, new field `ghost_and_stop_gates`):
  - The sword judge does nothing while the client is in ghost mode (`PLO_GHOSTMODE` 170).
  - Neither sword hits nor packet hits land while the client holds the flag that `PLO` 176 or 177 sets.
  - `Victim` and `OfflineSession` have new `ghost` / `ghost_mode` and `server_stop` fields. The app sets them from the session state.
- **Player mode 7** is a passenger on another local player's horse, in same-PC play. The 1.34 to 1.41 judge skips it, but that never applies online, so Seihai still doesn't test it.

## Respawn

- **1.39 revives with 3 hearts at every AP.** Before, it revived with full hearts. The revive routine sets 3.0 and reads no AP. The AP bands start in 1.41 rev 1. This replaces the "hearts to the maximum" in Graal_analysis `rules-139.md`.
