5.x builds now tell the server their nickname. In the seihai #400 lab run, 4.04r2 and 4.20r8 labelled a 5.00r7 or 5.12 player "default", and the gserver held `NICKNAME: default` for them. The cause: 5.x has no recorded post-login frame and no 4.x lobby frame, and the nickname-only `PLI_PLAYERPROPS` that 6.x sends after its first level was limited to 6.x.

## Fixes

- `sends_nick_props` is true from 5.00r7 on (it was 6.0r2 on). The 5.x session sends `PLI_PLAYERPROPS` with `NICKNAME` alone once its first level is in, as 6.x does.

No API changes.
