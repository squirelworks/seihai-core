The spark of a sword clash, and no binary addresses in the source.

## Breaking changes

- **`ObjectTables` and `OfflineSession` each have one more field**, `sword_spark`, and **`DrawTables` has `spark`** (the built-in table when the level's `arrays.dat` has none). A struct literal of any of them needs it.

## Baddies

- `baddies::SwordSpark`: the spark drawn on the local player's sword when it hits a baddy's sword. It lasts five 50 ms player ticks from the clash, and each tick draws one piece of `arrays.dat` array 21, picked by the player's facing and the frame. Every build from 1.28 beta 2 to 2.31 does the same.
- `World::sword_spark`, a default method, is called when the player's sword meets a baddy's sword. The offline session then keeps the spark in `OfflineSession::sword_spark` and ticks it.
- `ObjectTables::sword_spark` reads array 21.

## Source

- Client addresses are gone from the doc comments in `baddies.rs`, `offline/` and the GS2 builtins (`lists.rs`, `maths.rs`, `strings.rs`). Protocol constants and CRCs stay.
