# 0.4.4

`Script::write` no longer appends a trailing `0x0A` after the four-segment
GS2 container. Official server-compiled bodies end after segment 4; the
reader still ignores a leftover newline from older third-party compilers.
Fixture bodies and the opcode layout note match that shape.

Evidence: structural check of nineteen official 6.113-era bodies in
Graal_analysis `protocol/gs2-official-bytecode-6113.md` (no trailing
newline on any of them). No new lab run.
