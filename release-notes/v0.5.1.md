# 0.5.1

## GS1 to GS2 lowering (#93)

`putbomb` and `shoot` were `Missing`: the 3.153 client's GS2 table does not
register them, so a GS1 script using them did not compile to GS2. Real
compiled GS2 weapon scripts call both by name on later clients (Graal_analysis
`npc-library/gs2-weapon-corpus-2026-11-14.md`): `shoot` in 25 weapons of a
2007 world written for 4.20r8/5.00r7 and in 67 of a 2018 world, `putbomb` in
5 of the 2018 world. They now lower as `Same`, keeping the GS1 argument
order. Together they were 260 of the failing units in the #93 corpus run.
This is inferred from shipped scripts, not from a lab run.

`putexplosion`, `putexplosion2`, `puthorse`, `putnpc` and `putnpc2` stay
`Missing`: neither the 3.153 table nor any real GS2 script has them. A lab
check on a real GS2 client is tracked in #104.

## Release workflow (#78)

`release.yml` has a `macos` job that builds and tests the commit being
released on `macos-latest`, and the publish job needs it. CI on pushes and
PRs is unchanged (Linux and Windows).
