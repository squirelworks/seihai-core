GS1 player flags and four player built-ins are read by the GS2 names a real 5.24r4 client checked.

## Scripting

- In GS1 lowered to GS2, reading a player flag the script sets (`set gotkey;` then `if (gotkey)`) gives `player.gotkey`. Outside `with`, that is no longer an error.
- `playerhp` reads `player.hearts`, `playermaxhp` reads `player.fullhearts`, `weaponscount` reads `player.weapons.size()` and `selectedweapon` reads the GS2 `selectedweapon`. A lab probe of a 5.24r4 client gave the right values for each.
- Writing any of these, or reading a flag inside `with`, is still refused, and the error now says so.
- On the local corpus this brings back the 234 units #21 refused. No unit that already compiled changes its bytes.

## Checks

- `tests/gs2_modes.rs` and `tests/gs1_lowering_acceptance.rs` cover the new reads and the writes that are still refused.
