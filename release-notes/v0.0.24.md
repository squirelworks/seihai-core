Update cadence of 3.x, 4.x and 6.x measured: the 50 ms step holds.

## Research

- **`documentation/update-cadence.md`**: the Linux 3.04, 4.04r2, 4.20r6 and 6.03r7 clients and the Windows 4.04r2 under Wine all step the game every 50 ms (one render per step, 8 px a walk step, 10 tiles a second). This settles Graal_analysis E#301: Seihai's 50 ms tick, GANI timing and walk cadences are right for these builds too.

## Scripting

- The C++ engine (`Era::V304`, `Era::V3153`) counts a GS1 `timeout` in whole 50 ms steps, rounded down, and a timeout under one step never fires, as the real 3.04, 4.04r2 and 4.20r6 did (`0.049` silent, `0.07` every 50 ms, `0.12` every 100 ms, `0.15` every 150 ms). Other engines keep the round-up to at least one tick.

## Not done

- Falling behind is not modelled: the real 3.x/4.x clients slow down and 6.x catches up one step and drops the rest, where `ScriptWorld::tick` runs every missed tick.
