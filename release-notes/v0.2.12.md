Two GS1 lowering fixes from the first original-client run of Seihai's bytecode (#6).

## Changes

- **`setplayerprop` compiles to `player.*` assignments.** `setplayerprop #c,text` is now `player.chat = text`. `#n`, `#3`, `#8`, the colours and the attributes become `player.nick`, `player.headimg`, `player.bodyimg`, `player.colors[i]` and `player.attr[i]`. The real 6.03r7 has no `setplayerprop` function: it reported "Function setplayerprop not found" in the Mirae lab of 2026-11-14. Other codes still compile to the call by name.
- **`playerscount` and `npcscount`** compile to `players.size()` and `npcs.size()`. Left as plain names, they read as 0 on 6.03r7, so the lab's follow script never ran.

## Lab, 6.03r7

The lab ran on the Mirae Debian VM. A proxy put our bytecode into the client's NPC packet, with sha256 logged. These worked:

- the timer and its message;
- `setimg`;
- a text `showimg` with `client.` variables;
- the whole-script dispatcher, including its `else` branch.

These failed:

- `setplayerprop`, fixed above;
- the follow script, where the `playerscount` fix is the likely cause;
- `tiles[]` + `updateboard`, which showed no effect and no error and is still open.

The full table is in `documentation/lab/gs1-on-gs2-clients/lab-results.md` when the lab commits it.

No API changes.
