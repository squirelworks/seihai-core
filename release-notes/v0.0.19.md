Baddy, combat and tile rules that follow each client version, read from every binary from 1.21 to 2.31.

## Breaking changes

- **`CombatRules::lateral_below_half` is renamed `lateral_max_half`**, and the side limit now includes its edge (see Combat).
- **`CombatRules` has one more field**, `packet_hits`.
- **`VersionProfile` has one more field**, `world` (`WorldRules`).
- **`Baddy::sword_test` takes a `SwampSword`** as its last argument.

A struct literal of these needs the new fields; the `COMBAT_*` and `WORLD_*` constants fill them.

## Combat

- The sword box's side edge is inclusive: a target exactly 2 tiles to the side is hit. Every build compares `|side| <= 2.0`, so this is a fix for all versions.
- New rows `COMBAT_121` (1.21 to 1.33: no AP immunity) and `COMBAT_142` (NewMain 1.42). `COMBAT_139` now covers 1.34 to 1.41 and is built on the 2.00 sword judge (raw sword power, no once-a-second stamp).
- `CombatRules::packet_hits`: off for 1.x, where no handler for hits by packet was traced. `hurtplayer_message` sends none there, and the client reader decodes none.

## World

- `WorldRules` (`lava_burns`, `swamp_sword`) with `WORLD_121`, `WORLD_128` and `WORLD_201`. 1.21 has no lava burn. Up to 2.00 the sword reaches a surfacing swamp soldier from counter 17; from 2.01 only from 22 to 36.
- `OfflineSession::set_world_rules` takes a build's rules; `tick_lava` and the baddy sword test follow them.
- 1.21 to 1.41 baddies roll their own item when they start dying (`baddies::death_drop`: green or blue rupee, heart, or arrows from archers).

`documentation/version-chain.md` and `.tsv` and the golden profiles are regenerated.
