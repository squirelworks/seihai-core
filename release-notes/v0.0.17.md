Sound effects decoded by their contents: A-law, mu-law, MPEG audio and Ogg Vorbis.

## Sound

- `sound::decode_sound` reads a sound by its bytes, not its name: RIFF/WAVE, Ogg Vorbis, or MPEG audio (an ID3 tag or a frame sync). 6.x worlds ship an MP3 saved as `steps2.wav` (the 6.1.1.3 install, survey #446), and it now plays.
- `decode_wav` reads A-law and mu-law (format tags 6 and 7; 5.00r7's menu sounds) and MPEG audio inside a WAV (tag 0x55; 43 of 5.00r7's sounds).
- `decode_mp3` and `decode_ogg` are public. Ogg Vorbis effects play: `is_sound_name` and the engine take `.ogg` as an effect; `.mp3` and `.mid` by name are still music.
- The sound engine and the asset coverage check decode through `decode_sound`.

## Dependencies

- `lewton` 0.10 (Ogg Vorbis) and `nanomp3` 0.2 (MPEG audio), both pure Rust, MIT OR Apache-2.0.
