The shared client session hands the game what Ato and Issho need for baddies and for other players' carried objects, and the 6.x GS2 scripts and music make sound (squirelworks/seihai#497, #446).

## Additions

- `client::Live::leader`: set while the server has made the player the level's leader (`PLO_ISLEADER`), who runs the level's baddies.
- `client::Other::carry`: what another player holds over the head (CARRYSPRITE, 19), so a view can draw it.
- `sound::SoundEngine::music()` and `stop_music()`.

## Changes

- `client::Live::level_objects` now carries the baddies of the player's level too: `PLO_BADDYPROPS` (on a gmap only the player's own level's), `PLO_BADDYHURT` and the clear on a new level. They were dropped before. A view that matched only the bomb and arrow kinds (with a `_` arm) is unaffected.
- The GS2 `EffectHost` turns `play`, `playlooped`, `play2`, `stopsound` and `stopmidi` into the GS1 engine's `Effect::Play` / `StopSound` / `StopMidi`, so a GS2 script's sound reaches the session's sound queue as a GS1 script's does. They were generic `Effect::Command`s, which nothing played.
- `SoundEngine` plays `.mp3` music (the 6.x worlds' music) on one slot of its own: centred, outside the eight effect voices, one piece at a time, and `stopsound` ends it. How FMOD mixes the 6.x client's music was not read, so that slot is Seihai's. `is_sound_name` takes `.mp3`, so a missing piece is asked for with `WANTFILE`. MIDI (`.mid`) is still skipped with one note: there is no synthesizer.

## Compatibility

`Live` and `Other` gain a field each. Every caller in the suite builds them with `..Default::default()` (or `Live::default()`), which keeps compiling, so this is a patch release.
